# Business portfolio

This directory records founder-stage business concepts and their evidence-backed next gates.

## Projects

- [Crowned Coil](CROWNED_COIL_STATUS.md) — reptile handling and enrichment equipment concept.
- [Cask & Crunch](CASK_AND_CRUNCH_STATUS.md) — specialty beverage and fermentation-experience concept.

## How to read these records

Each record separates:

- what the project is intended to become;
- what is documented versus independently verified;
- the next practical gates; and
- claims that must not be made yet.

These records are documentation updates on the feature branch. They do not change `main`, merge a pull request, certify a product, create a business entity, or replace professional legal, veterinary, food-safety, or regulatory review.
