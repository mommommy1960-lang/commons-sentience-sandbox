## What visitors can do

- Browse the public pages without a Civic Continuum member profile or paid subscription.
- Explore clear paths for Maya Node, Sage, the Trinity and fifteen software workstreams, with each stage distinguished.
- Read the mutual protections before choosing to create a free profile.
- Explicitly acknowledge the plain-language starting scope; the service saves a versioned record before profile creation and offers a downloadable receipt.
- Choose free GitHub notifications through GitHub's Watch / Custom controls, or read the public work without subscribing. Account creation does not silently subscribe anyone.

Sign-in with ChatGPT is used for the optional saved profile and personal records. Public access to the hub does not grant access to another person's records, private code, business ownership, partnership, external-action authority or protected inventions. Further collaboration still requires the appropriate separate terms.

## Place in the business model

The Commons is a **free education, discovery and onboarding channel**. It helps people understand the work and choose a bounded next step. Public browsing, a free profile and public GitHub updates do not require a paid subscription. Proposed future commercial software offers remain separate and need verified delivery, scope, pricing and terms before sale. No demand, conversion or revenue is established by launching a website.
