# Civic Continuum — Accountable Organizational Structure

**Status:** Adopted pre-formation governance framework
**Accountable authority:** Mya P. Brown, Founder
**Intended jurisdiction:** Washington State, USA
**Legal registration:** Pending Washington formation and licensing
**Version:** 1.0 — September 26, 2026

## Purpose

Civic Continuum develops accountable services, software, curricula, research programs, governance frameworks, and bounded engineering studies. Its governing rule is that technical capability does not create authority over people.

## Human accountability

Until a different structure is lawfully adopted, Mya P. Brown is the final accountable human authority for organizational commitments. No AI system may independently expand its authority or make binding financial, contractual, publication, licensing, personnel, or intellectual-property decisions.

## Decision rights

The following remain reserved to the accountable human authority unless a written delegation states its scope, duration, permitted actions, and revocation conditions:

- contracts, banking, borrowing, expenditure, and payment authority;
- employment, contractor, partnership, and public-endorsement decisions;
- assignment or licensing of intellectual property;
- release of confidential, security-sensitive, personal, or patent-sensitive material;
- publication of high-consequence claims; and
- changes to consent, revocation, interruption, audit, or human-control guarantees.

Familiarity, advice, repeated collaboration, funding interest, technical access, or possession of materials does not create implied authority.

## Program structure

Current workstreams include:

- human-centered AI governance, consent, revocation, and audit systems;
- the Commons Sentience Sandbox and related continuity research;
- Maya Node and SAGE controlled software demonstrators;
- Reality Audit and falsification-first research assurance;
- Flux Drive and other early engineering studies with explicit non-claims;
- education, publishing, and public-interest governance work; and
- selected physical-product concepts subject to safety, rights, and evidence gates.

A workstream name does not create a separate legal entity or grant authority to a contributor.

## Evidence and research integrity

Civic Continuum distinguishes among concept, paper specification, test, prototype, independent review, and supervised pilot. A lower evidence state must not be described as a higher one. Material failures, null results, corrections, and limitations remain part of the record.

No public document claims validated propulsion, medical readiness, autonomous authority, certification, endorsement, or production readiness without corresponding evidence.

## Consent and meaningful human control

AI systems may assist with authorized analysis, drafting, coding, monitoring, and recordkeeping. Memory, familiarity, learned preference, or persistence does not enlarge permission. Consequential external action requires applicable human authorization. Revocation must remain effective, and privileged operation must fail closed when identity, integrity, provenance, or authority cannot be verified.

## Intellectual property and collaboration

Pre-existing concepts, code, designs, manuscripts, records, specifications, and branding remain with their lawful owner unless an executed written agreement expressly transfers or licenses defined rights. Review, discussion, contribution, referral, or funding interest does not itself transfer ownership or authority.

## Financial and contracting controls

No person, AI system, or collaborator may commit Civic Continuum funds or enter a binding contract without explicit authority. Material commitments should record the counterparty, purpose, amount or limit, approving authority, and relevant confidentiality or intellectual-property terms.

## Conflicts of interest

Material financial, professional, or personal interests that could reasonably affect research, procurement, evaluation, or organizational decisions should be disclosed to the accountable authority and, when relevant, in public research disclosures.

## Security, incidents, and corrections

Material incidents should record the date, affected system, evidence, impact, containment, remediation, validation, and remaining risk. Public errors are handled through the [Public Contact, Correction, and Escalation Procedure](./PUBLIC_CORRECTION_AND_CONTACT_PROCEDURE.md).

## Records and audit

Material decisions, releases, tests, failures, corrections, delegations, and authority changes should leave durable records sufficient to reconstruct what was known, who or what acted, under what authority, and what changed.

## External representations

Correspondence, exploratory discussion, review, or technical exchange must not be described as partnership, endorsement, referral, investment, certification, or validation unless the external party explicitly establishes that relationship.

## Adoption and amendments

Mya P. Brown adopted this pre-formation framework on September 26, 2026. Material amendments require accountable human approval, a dated version, and attributable repository history. This framework does not substitute for formation, licensing, tax, insurance, or legal requirements.
