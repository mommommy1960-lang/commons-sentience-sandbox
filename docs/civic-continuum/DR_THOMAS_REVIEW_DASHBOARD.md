## Evidence meter

- `[1/6] Concept` — defined idea or source record
- `[2/6] Paper` — bounded specification, assumptions, and non-claims
- `[3/6] Test` — reproducible test plan and pass/fail/stop criteria
- `[4/6] Prototype` — software, tabletop, or bench artifact
- `[5/6] Review` — independent technical, safety, legal, or rights review
- `[6/6] Pilot` — supervised real-world use with support and correction procedures

A project may move backward when testing exposes a flaw. That is recorded as progress in the evidence system, not hidden.

## What a serious reviewer can do next

1. Inspect the portfolio index and registry.
2. Select one bounded workstream.
3. Review its assumptions, non-claims, tests, and failure criteria.
4. Request the private material only through an appropriate NDA or review process.
5. Reproduce the public software or analysis where possible.
6. Report disagreements and failed replications.
7. Fund or partner on the next decisive gate—not on an unsupported end-state claim.

## Important boundary

Flux Drive is not independently validated propulsion. Aurora is not a validated flying city-ship. Biomedical concepts are not medical devices. SAGE is not a production-ready autonomous companion. The value of this repository is the visible path from ambitious concept to falsifiable, reviewable work.
