# Civic Continuum Curriculum Proof

## Purpose

This is the first practical proof: make one documented curriculum teachable, auditable, and useful.

## Verified subject

The curriculum is for the Socio-Technical Systems Accountability Engineer. It teaches learners to:

- distinguish intent from outcome;
- identify moral disengagement and role diffusion;
- analyze enforcement pipelines as structural violence;
- translate lived experience into evidence;
- map roles, incentives, artifacts, and feedback loops;
- design accountability architectures;
- use freeze and pause controls when verification fails.
