# Civic Continuum Public Contact, Correction, and Escalation Procedure

**Effective date:** September 26, 2026  
**Public contact:** `contact@civiccontinuum.org`

## Purpose

This procedure gives the public, contributors, reviewers, and affected people a stable way to report an error, disputed claim, attribution concern, safety issue, privacy concern, or request for correction.

## What to include

A useful request identifies:

1. the page, file, statement, or decision at issue;
2. the reason it may be inaccurate, incomplete, unsafe, improperly attributed, or misleading;
3. supporting evidence or a source link when available; and
4. the correction or review requested.

Do not email passwords, authentication codes, recovery codes, Social Security numbers, financial-account data, medical records, or unpublished patent-enabling details.

## Intake and acknowledgement

Requests sent to `contact@civiccontinuum.org` enter the designated administrative inbox. The responsible owner records the date, affected workstream, issue type, and current status. When a reply address is available, Civic Continuum aims to acknowledge ordinary requests within five business days.

## Review levels

| Level | Example | Required action |
|---|---|---|
| Routine | Typographical error, broken link, formatting issue | Verify and correct through version history |
| Substantive | Factual error, missing limitation, disputed attribution, unsupported claim | Preserve the original record, review evidence, publish a reasoned correction or explanation |
| Urgent | Safety risk, exposed private data, credential leak, active legal deadline | Limit exposure, preserve necessary evidence privately, notify the responsible owner immediately, and escalate to qualified help when required |

## Correction record

A material correction should identify:

- what changed;
- why it changed;
- the date of the change;
- the responsible decision maker; and
- the supporting evidence or limitation.

Repository history must not be used to conceal an earlier material error. Sensitive information may be removed from public view while a private incident record is retained when lawful and appropriate.

## Disagreement and appeal

If the requester disagrees with the outcome, they may reply with new evidence and request a second review. Until another accountable reviewer is formally appointed, the responsible owner records both the original decision and the unresolved disagreement. No response may claim independent review when none occurred.

## Confidential and patent-sensitive material

The public contact route is not an NDA channel. Civic Continuum will not ask a person to place trade secrets, unpublished patent-enabling details, credentials, or regulated personal information in ordinary email. A controlled process must be established before such material is exchanged.

## Demonstration record

The domain contact route was tested successfully on September 26, 2026. The private destination inbox is not published. This test verifies message delivery only; it does not establish legal formation, attorney representation, endorsement, or independent validation.
