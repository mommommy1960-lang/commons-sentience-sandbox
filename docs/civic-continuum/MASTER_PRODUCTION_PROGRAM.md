# Civic Continuum Master Production Program

Civic Continuum is the product, research, education, and public-interest operating brand for the Commons Initiative.

This document is the control layer for moving the Commons corpus from archived concepts to useful, tested, lawful, revenue-producing products.

## Operating rule

Reality first. Anomaly second. Follow through.

Every project must be classified as PRODUCT, PROTOTYPE, RESEARCH, PUBLISHING, or HOLD.

A Zenodo DOI proves that a record was archived. It does not prove feasibility, patent rights, safety, accreditation, revenue, or readiness for sale.

## First revenue sequence

Prioritize low-cost digital products and services before physical manufacturing:

1. Maya Node human-delivered AI consent and control reviews.
2. Civic Continuum governance briefs, checklists, and templates.
3. Civic Continuum Companion software demonstrations and paid pilots.
4. Housing ledger and systems-accountability training products.
5. Crowned Coil founder preorders only after prototype validation.

## Portfolio crosswalk

### Maya Node — PRODUCT

Offer: bounded human-delivered AI consent, permission-boundary, auditability, and adversarial design review.

Customer: founders, builders, and researchers.

Revenue: founding review and later professional tiers.

Proof required: intake, scope form, report template, adversarial test, disclaimer, payment path, and completed client delivery.

### Civic Continuum Companion — PROTOTYPE

Offer: safe phone-connected companion software with inspectable memory, explicit permissions, bounded work, emergency freeze, and honest activity reports.

Customer: creators, researchers, accessibility users, and carefully selected pilot users.

Proof required: passing tests, working runtime contract, memory correction/deletion/export, permission boundaries, freeze, shutdown, rollback, audit integrity, local install package, and bounded independent review.

No sentience, consciousness, or unrestricted autonomy claim.

### Governance and public-interest products — PRODUCT/PUBLISHING

Products:

- plain-English AI governance brief;
- consent and permission checklist;
- audit-sidecar template;
- institutional-harm artifact worksheet;
- housing-ledger training module;
- systems-accountability starter course;
- housing-pipeline diagnostic.

Proof required: finished deliverable, source notes, price, delivery path, privacy review, and no unsupported legal claim.

### Civic Continuum Academy — PUBLISHING

Offer: courses in systems accountability, governance, ledger analysis, and institutional design.

Revenue: digital courses, workshops, memberships, and institutional contracts.

Proof required: curriculum, exercises, assessment, completion criteria, instructor notes, and transparent non-accreditation language unless formal accreditation exists.

### Crowned Coil — PROTOTYPE

Start with one signature serpent hook and target system.

Required before inventory:

- itemized supplier quote;
- drawings or CAD;
- materials and finish;
- safety limitations distinguishing design guidance from tested ratings;
- prototype photographs;
- supervised keeper-handling test;
- packaging and fulfillment estimate;
- written approval before paid fabrication.

### Cask & Crunch — PRODUCT/VENTURE

Start with a paid tasting, pop-up, or packaged pairing test only after food-safety, alcohol licensing, location, and cost checks.

The concept is not a licensed operating business until those requirements are satisfied.

### C.A.R.E. rescue systems — RESEARCH/PROTOTYPE

Select one component with a narrow, testable claim. Do not begin with the full rescue ecosystem or life-support claims.

Required proof: engineering review, materials testing, human-factors review, supervised evaluation, and documented failure limits.

### Reality Audit — RESEARCH/SOFTWARE

Offer: falsification-first analysis toolkit and reproducible public-data workflow.

Proof required: null models, blinded analysis where appropriate, reproducible tests, preserved failure reasons, and independent review.

### Commons Sentience Sandbox — RESEARCH/SOFTWARE

Offer: governed-agent benchmark for continuity, memory, contradiction handling, and bounded behavior.

The project explicitly does not claim sentience, consciousness, or AGI.

### Orbital stewardship, ORBICYCLE, and OSH-1 — RESEARCH

Offer: standards, simulations, governance analysis, and systems architecture.

Proof required: high-fidelity simulation, engineering review, legal authorization analysis, and orbital demonstration before operational claims.

### Aurora, Flux Drive, QCV, and MPRS — HOLD/RESEARCH

Extract and commercialize only conventional submodules and falsification studies until evidence crosses defined gates.

Required evidence for propulsion claims includes measured momentum closure, orientation reversals, sham controls, environmental-systematics analysis, hardware-in-loop testing, and independent review.

## Definition of better

Civic Continuum competes through visible evidence, honest limits, lower harm, user control, beautiful design, affordability, repairability, completion, public accountability, and measurable outcomes.

We do not claim superiority before testing. We make superiority a result to be demonstrated.

## Production gates

No project is launch-ready until it has:

- a defined user and problem;
- a supportable claim boundary;
- a reproducible build or production process;
- safety and privacy documentation;
- tests or external evidence appropriate to the product;
- a price and fulfillment path;
- an owner and maintenance plan;
- a public record of unresolved limitations.
