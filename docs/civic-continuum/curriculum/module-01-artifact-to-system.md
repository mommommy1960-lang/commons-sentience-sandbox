# Module 01 Artifact to System

## Course

Socio-Technical Systems Accountability Engineering

## Learning objectives

By the end of this 60-minute module, the learner can:

1. separate an observed outcome from an institution's stated intent;
2. identify the artifacts that carry evidence;
3. map actors, incentives, decisions, delays, and feedback loops;
4. state one uncertainty and one correction question without assigning unsupported motive.

## Materials

Use one redacted, fictionalized administrative packet containing a notice, ledger excerpt, timeline, and response record. Do not use private identifying information in a classroom packet.

## Procedure

1. **Observe — 10 minutes:** list only what each artifact says or records.
2. **Normalize — 10 minutes:** build a date-ordered timeline and mark missing records.
3. **Map — 15 minutes:** connect roles, incentives, artifacts, decisions, delays, and consequences.
4. **Test — 15 minutes:** identify the smallest claim that can be checked and one alternative explanation.
5. **Correct — 10 minutes:** write one record-ready question that requests a specific correction or accountable response.

## Learner submission

- artifact inventory;
- timeline;
- system map;
- uncertainty statement;
- one correction request.

## Assessment rubric

- Evidence fidelity: 0–2
- Timeline completeness: 0–2
- System mapping: 0–2
- Uncertainty discipline: 0–2
- Correction specificity: 0–2

A score of 8 or higher means the learner completed this module. It is not professional certification.

## Safety boundary

Use redacted or synthetic records. Do not diagnose a person's motives, publish personal information, or provide legal advice. Refer legal or clinical questions to qualified professionals.
