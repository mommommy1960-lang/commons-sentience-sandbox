# Speculative Research Edition

## Publication boundary

The speculative papers are valuable as disciplined questions and design fiction. They are not presented as demonstrated physics, completed human-subject research, or validated consciousness engineering.

## Translation table

| Source phrase | Research-safe formulation |
|---|---|
| Quantum emotional interface | Consent-governed multimodal affective interface |
| Neural entanglement | Secure distributed state synchronization |
| Moral entanglement | Measured policy agreement among autonomous agents |
| Collective consciousness | Consent-governed collaborative cognition |
| Reality shield | Sensor integrity, provenance, misinformation resistance, and cognitive security |
| Soul print | User-controlled identity and continuity record |
| Temporal ethics | Governance of predictions, simulations, delayed effects, and historical records |
| Planetary consciousness link | Ecological sensor fusion and participatory planetary modeling |

## Falsifiable research questions

1. Can multimodal affect classifiers communicate calibrated uncertainty and reduce harmful overconfidence compared with a speech-only baseline?
2. Can independent agents reach policy agreement while preserving withdrawal, privacy budgets, and minority objections?
3. Can users detect, inspect, and revoke synthetic emotional feedback without losing access to the underlying service?
4. Can provenance and sensor cross-checks reduce manipulation without suppressing legitimate disagreement or neurodivergence?
5. Can ecological sensor networks support useful collective decisions without describing the network as conscious?

## Prohibited claims until evidence exists

- faster-than-light communication through entanglement;
- physical encoding of morality in a wavefunction;
- experimental temporal control;
- verified synthetic consciousness;
- verified human-machine consciousness merging; or
- communication with a conscious planet.

Negative results remain publishable outcomes.
