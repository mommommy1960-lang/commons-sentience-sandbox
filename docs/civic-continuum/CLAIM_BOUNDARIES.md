# Civic Continuum claim boundaries

## Why this exists

The Commons Initiative can be ambitious without presenting hypotheses as facts. This document is a release-control rule for websites, papers, code, sales pages, funding materials, and public statements.

## Allowed language

Use precise language such as proposed, simulated, scaffolded, draft, literature-informed, under test, not independently validated, or human-delivered. State what was actually measured, by whom, under what conditions, and what remains unknown.

## Prohibited overclaiming

Do not claim sentience, consciousness, universal autonomy, self-awareness, magical security, perfect alignment, working quantum propulsion, inertial cancellation, interstellar capability, medical efficacy, emergency-dispatch readiness, legal enforceability by code, or revenue until the relevant evidence exists.

Do not describe a DOI as a patent, patent grant, legal monopoly, or proof that an invention works. A timestamp can support provenance; it does not establish technical validity or ownership against every later claimant.

Do not describe a cryptographic control as autonomous law enforcement. Cryptography can authenticate, detect, log, and provide evidence. Courts, contracts, platforms, and people provide legal enforcement.

## Required product disclosures

Every product page must state:

1. what the product does;
2. what it does not do;
3. who controls permissions;
4. what data it stores or transmits;
5. how a user pauses, corrects, exports, or deletes data;
6. known failure modes;
7. validation status;
8. price and refund terms when applicable;
9. support and escalation path;
10. date and version of the disclosure.

## Safety boundaries

The Companion is non-medical and does not autonomously control vehicles, weapons, financial accounts, utilities, or other critical infrastructure. Maya Node is a design review, not legal advice, certification, penetration testing, or a security guarantee. Crowned Coil products require animal-safety testing and explicit use limits. Research programs must remain labeled as research.

## Evidence record

Before a claim is promoted, record the claim, evidence source, test method, result, limitations, reviewer, and date in GitHub. When evidence fails, preserve the failure and revise the claim rather than deleting the record.
