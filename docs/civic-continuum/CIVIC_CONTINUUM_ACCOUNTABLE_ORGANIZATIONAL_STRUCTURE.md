# Civic Continuum Accountable Organizational Structure

**Effective date:** September 26, 2026  
**Status:** Public interim structure pending formal Washington entity formation  
**Responsible owner:** Mya P. Brown

## Purpose

This document identifies who may make decisions for Civic Continuum, how authority is bounded, how records are preserved, and how concerns may be raised. It is an interim public accountability record. It does not claim that Civic Continuum is already a legally formed entity.

## Current authority and roles

| Role | Named person | Current authority | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder and responsible owner | Mya P. Brown | Sets organizational direction; approves public claims, releases, partnerships, spending, and formal submissions | Must preserve evidence boundaries, applicable law, consent, safety review, and correction records |
| Project steward | Mya P. Brown until a later written appointment | Maintains project registers, evidence status, decision records, and public/private boundaries | Cannot convert an unverified result into a validated claim |
| Technical contributor | Named per repository contribution record | May propose, document, test, and review work within an assigned scope | No independent authority to bind Civic Continuum or represent approval |
| External reviewer or adviser | Named only through a written record | May examine a defined question and report findings | Review does not create ownership, endorsement, employment, or decision authority |
| Delegate | None currently appointed | No delegated authority exists unless a dated written appointment defines it | Authority expires or may be revoked under the appointment terms |

## Decision rights

The responsible owner must approve:

1. legal filings and contracts;
2. financial commitments;
3. patent, trademark, licensing, or confidential-disclosure decisions;
4. public claims of validation, readiness, safety, partnership, or endorsement;
5. access to private identity, security, patent-strategy, or supplier information; and
6. appointment or removal of delegates.

Routine documentation and testing may continue without a separate resolution when it stays within published evidence and safety boundaries.

## Evidence and claims rule

Civic Continuum distinguishes concept, paper, test, prototype, independent review, and supervised pilot. A claim may not be promoted to a later stage without inspectable evidence. Negative findings, failed tests, uncertainty, and conflicts must remain in the record.

## Conflicts of interest

A person reviewing work must disclose any financial, ownership, family, supervisory, or competitive interest that could affect judgment. A material conflict is recorded and the affected person does not make the final decision on that matter unless no alternative exists and the limitation is disclosed.

## Records and change control

Material decisions must have a dated repository record, signed resolution, or controlled administrative record. Public files use version history. Private records containing credentials, addresses, tax identifiers, income documents, signatures, recovery codes, or patent-sensitive material remain access-controlled.

## Delegation, revocation, and succession

Delegated authority must identify the person, scope, start date, expiration or review date, and revocation method. Mya P. Brown may revoke a delegation in writing. A permanent succession plan will be adopted after legal formation; no successor is presently appointed.

## Contact and correction

Public questions and correction requests may be sent to `contact@civiccontinuum.org`. The handling procedure is published in [Public Contact, Correction, and Escalation Procedure](./CIVIC_CONTINUUM_PUBLIC_CORRECTION_PROCEDURE.md).

## Adoption boundary

This interim structure is publicly adopted by Mya P. Brown as the founder's operating accountability record on September 26, 2026. Formal legal adoption, officer or manager appointments, and succession instruments remain pending until entity formation.
