# Civic Continuum master portfolio crosswalk

## Purpose

This document maps the broad Commons Initiative archive into buildable workstreams. A paper is not automatically a product. Each workstream must earn its status through evidence, tests, safety review, rights review, and a usable release.

## Product and research lanes

### Civic Continuum Companion

Primary near-term software product. Build a bounded phone-connected companion with explicit permissions, inspectable memory, continuity records, a job queue, emergency freeze, and an honest activity report. It is non-medical and does not receive autonomous control over vehicles, weapons, finances, or critical infrastructure.

### Maya Node

Primary near-term service product. Sell human-delivered AI design reviews focused on consent, authority boundaries, freshness, revocation, auditability, and fail-closed behavior. Deliverables are a written risk report and one practical adversarial test. It is not certification, legal advice, penetration testing, or proof of sentience.

### Governance and public-interest software

Convert Systems Accountability Engineering, Institutionalizing Common Sense, Anti-Capture Governance, and related housing work into evidence-led templates, case-study tools, audit checklists, training materials, and paid review services. No unsupported legal conclusions; jurisdiction-specific legal work requires qualified counsel.

### Civic Continuum Academy

Educational products derived from the governance corpus: short courses, workshops, facilitator guides, and public explainers. Claims must distinguish original analysis, cited evidence, and hypotheses.

### Crowned Coil

Physical product lane after safety validation. Start with one reptile-safe handling-tool prototype, local fabrication, documented load and use limits, supervised user testing, and a small founder release. No inventory or supplier payment without written approval.

### Cask & Crunch

Hospitality concept lane. Keep at planning and compliance validation until location, licensing, food safety, alcohol rules, capital, and operating economics are verified.

### C.A.R.E. and emergency-response concepts

Treat as research and workflow-design candidates. First release should be a non-clinical, human-supervised information and coordination tool. No medical or emergency dispatch claims without domain validation and regulatory review.

### Reality Audit and Sentience Sandbox

Research infrastructure. Publish reproducible methods, null models, limitations, and code status. Do not market simulation claims, sentience claims, or metaphysical interpretations as established findings.

### Orbital, Aurora, Flux Drive, QCV, and MPRS concepts

Research or hold. Extract ordinary-physics software, systems-engineering, simulation, and governance modules only. Propulsion, interstellar, quantum-consciousness, and other unsupported physical claims do not become commercial product claims without independent scientific evidence.

### Publishing

Use rights-cleared original works for the initial publishing queue. DC-character works remain unpublished until written permission or a rights-cleared revision is documented.

## Evidence labels

- PRODUCT: usable offer with validated scope and a delivery path.
- PROTOTYPE: working implementation or physical sample under test.
- SERVICE: human-delivered work with clear boundaries and price approval.
- RESEARCH: hypothesis, model, simulation, or literature-grounded proposal.
- PUBLISHING: rights-cleared document or educational release.
- HOLD: blocked by safety, evidence, legal, funding, or rights constraints.

## Promotion rule

A workstream moves forward only when its acceptance evidence exists in GitHub: source or manuscript, tests or review checklist, claim boundaries, risk log, and a dated decision record.
