# Civic Continuum Paper Market Readiness Program

## Objective

Convert the existing work into accurate, rights-cleared, customer-facing paper
packages without claiming that concepts, simulations, or archived documents are
validated physical products.

## Market wave one

### Maya Node Founding Review

**Customer:** founders, builders, and researchers seeking a second look at consent,
authority, auditability, and human-control boundaries.

**Offer:** one written boundary-risk report and one bounded adversarial test.

**Status:** offer exists; repeatable fulfillment packet remains the immediate task.

**Required paper assets:** service page, intake form, scope and disclaimer, report
template, adversarial-test template, delivery checklist, correction path, privacy note,
and feedback request.

**Claims excluded:** certification, legal advice, penetration testing, production
approval, sentience determination, or guarantee of safety.

### Consent Token demonstrator

**Customer:** organizations exploring consent-aware automation and auditability.

**Offer:** a supervised demonstration of grant, deny, expiry, revoke, freeze, restore,
replay protection, and audit verification.

**Status:** paper architecture and proof-cycle target; external review automation may
remain blocked by missing credentials or billing.

**Release gate:** secret-independent deterministic tests must pass locally before any
public product claim.

### Systems Accountability Engineering module

**Customer:** housing organizations, nonprofits, auditors, educators, and mission-
aligned employers.

**Offer:** one bounded 90-minute pilot module with learning objectives, exercises,
facilitator notes, accessibility provisions, safety boundaries, and assessment rubric.

**Status:** source material exists; module still requires consolidation, pilot review,
and learner feedback.

### Selected books

The first paper-market sequence is *Failure Without Malice*, followed by
*Practicing Violence*, then carefully reviewed companion works. Each requires final
editing, source and rights review, cover and metadata verification, accessible proof,
and a clear distinction between testimony, analysis, satire, fiction, and policy.

## Engineering paper packages

The first physical invention briefs should be prepared for Crowned Coil, AQUASHIELD,
the low-energy measurement bench, and the programmable vehicle surface. Each brief
must include the problem, requirements, ordinary mechanism, assumptions, hazards,
failure modes, materials, test plan, cost range, independent reviewer, regulatory
boundary, stop condition, and commercial route.

## Protection and disclosure rule

No supplier receives protected drawings or paid design authorization until the NDA,
ownership, confidentiality, publication, payment, liability, and commercialization
terms are reviewed. Public GitHub receives sanitized summaries only. Defensive
publication is not a substitute for patent advice and does not prevent copying.

## Business operating sequence

1. Choose one customer and one narrow offer.
2. Set a truthful price hypothesis and payment path.
3. Deliver one supervised pilot.
4. Record time, cost, defects, questions, and customer outcome.
5. Correct the offer and documentation.
6. Repeat with a second customer.
7. Publish an evidence summary with failures and limitations.
8. Only then expand, license, manufacture, or seek larger capital.

## Stop conditions

Pause a project when evidence is missing, safety review fails, rights are unclear,
costs exceed available resources, customer need is unconfirmed, or the claim requires
unsupported physics, unapproved medical work, or unsafe experimentation.
