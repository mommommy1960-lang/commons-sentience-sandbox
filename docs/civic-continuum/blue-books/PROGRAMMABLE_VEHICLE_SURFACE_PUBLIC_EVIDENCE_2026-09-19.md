# Programmable Vehicle Surface Public Evidence Record

Date: 2026-09-19  
Creator: Mya P. Brown  
Project family: AURORA VISAGE, CHROMASKIN, and AURORA SURFACE OS

## Public concept boundary

The project investigates a modular, serviceable optical surface that can alter the visible appearance of a stationary panel and, only after appropriate testing and regulatory work, may be evaluated for future vehicle applications. A controller and diagnostic layer would manage displayed content, calibration, module identity, fault reporting, privacy controls, and safe fallback behavior.

This record does not disclose protected construction drawings, material stack details, routing geometry, unpublished implementation methods, or candidate patent-claim language.

## First physical evidence gate

The first article is a stationary 600 mm by 600 mm coupon. It is not a complete car covering and must not be operated on a public road.

The coupon should be judged against preregistered tests for:

1. Repeatable commanded visual states.
2. Optical uniformity under multiple viewing angles and lighting conditions.
3. Measured electrical power and surface temperature.
4. Graceful behavior during power loss, controller fault, and disconnected-module conditions.
5. Bend and mounting tolerance appropriate to the selected materials.
6. Replaceability of a damaged module without replacing the entire coupon.
7. Water, dust, ultraviolet, vibration, and temperature-cycle resistance appropriate to the intended demonstration environment.
8. Photographic and instrumented evidence sufficient for independent inspection.

## Commercial boundary

Before the evidence gate is passed, the defensible commercial offering is a commissioned stationary demonstrator, exhibit, design study, or prototype-development service. It is not yet a road-safe exterior product. Any automotive use requires additional testing, legal review, insurance, electromagnetic compatibility review, driver-distraction analysis, visibility and lighting compliance, mounting validation, and relevant transportation approvals.

## Prior art and ownership

The general idea of changeable vehicle appearance has prior art. U.S. Patent No. 9,927,087 was identified during preliminary review. Novelty, patentability, validity, infringement, and freedom to operate have not been determined. Mya P. Brown reports sole conception of the project features documented in her private record; formal inventorship remains claim-specific and must be evaluated against any eventual patent claims and contributions.

## Current status

Documentation and prototype planning are complete enough to begin a low-risk stationary coupon design review. Materials selection, bill of materials, test fixtures, fabrication, measurements, and independent replication remain open.
