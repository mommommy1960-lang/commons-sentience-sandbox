# Civic Continuum Revenue Proof

## Purpose

Create one repeatable, ethical revenue path that supports the work.

## Candidate lanes

- Maya Node founding reviews;
- plain-English AI governance briefs;
- supervised systems-accountability workshops;
- curriculum workbooks and reader editions;
- Crowned Coil only after quotes, NDA review, safety testing, and approved scope.

## Revenue test

For one offer, record:

- target user;
- problem solved;
- price;
- delivery time;
- direct cost;
- refund or correction policy;
- evidence of user value;
- next improvement.

Revenue must not depend on overstating scientific readiness, claiming certification, or withholding basic safety information.

## Gate

Complete a small number of paid or donated pilot deliveries, document outcomes honestly, and decide whether the offer is repeatable.
