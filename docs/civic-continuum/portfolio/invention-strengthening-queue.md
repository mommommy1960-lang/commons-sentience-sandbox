## Tier 2 Strengthen after Tier 1

- Emergency Response System: define a narrow dispatch-support use case, human override, audit trail, accessibility requirements, and simulated evaluation.
- Plain-English AI governance briefs and workbooks: convert the strongest modules into a short paid product with rights, privacy, and evidence review.
- Cask and Crunch: validate location, licensing, costs, demand, and a small event or pop-up before a permanent venue.
- Reality Audit: preserve the falsification boundary, preregistration, null models, blinding, and independent review. Do not market it as evidence that reality is simulated.

## Research-only until independent evidence

- Flux Drive and Aurora propulsion: no propulsion or FTL claim; focus on measurement-grade bench tests, momentum closure, error budgets, and falsification.
- Orbital debris and OSH-1 or ORBICYCLE: architecture and simulation only until authorization, high-fidelity modeling, hardware tests, and orbital partners exist.
- LQIDA and quantum engineering: reduce claims to measurable noise suppression; require calibrated instrumentation and independent replication.
- Bio-Regenerative Limbs, engineered-life, bio-archival, and syntropic biology: research concepts only; require qualified institutional, medical, biological, environmental, and legal review.
- FOS, SLP, H3DFS, and licensing infrastructure: strengthen conventional provenance, reproducible builds, access control, and transparent governance; avoid claims of magical immutability or autonomous legal enforcement.

## Stage-gate rule

A project advances only when it has:

1. a defined user or research question;
2. a bounded claim;
3. an inspectable artifact;
4. a safety and rights review;
5. a measurable test;
6. a cost and capacity estimate;
7. a stop or revise condition;
8. a public evidence record.
