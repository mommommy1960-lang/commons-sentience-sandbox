### The problem

Automation can save time, but it can also create serious problems when a system acts without clear, current human authorization. A business needs to know what was requested, what was approved, what happened, and where a person must review or correct the result.

### The idea behind Maya Node

Maya Node is being developed around one central principle:

> **Technical capability does not confer authority.**

Its purpose is to make permission boundaries and action records easier for people to inspect. The current work is a bounded prototype and demonstration, not a finished general-purpose automation service.

### What we are evaluating

Civic Continuum is inviting one small business or independent professional practice to an exploratory conversation about a single, low-risk administrative workflow. A founder-guided demonstration may be considered using invented information only.

The discussion can help:
- Map the steps in an existing administrative process.
- Identify where authorization or human review may be missing.
- Consider duplicate, expired, revoked, or otherwise unauthorized requests.
- Examine available sample decision and activity receipts, with their test environment identified.
- Identify practical safeguards and opportunities for improvement.

### Current limitations

Maya Node is still in development. Current demonstrations do not establish a fully operational customer-facing automation service. We are not offering autonomous operation of external applications, live automated email dispatch, unrestricted AI-generated responses, production integrations, or regulatory compliance certification.

### An invitation to discuss

We are interested in speaking with one small business willing to discuss a low-risk administrative workflow. This is an exploratory conversation—not a request for confidential records, live automation authority, or payment.
