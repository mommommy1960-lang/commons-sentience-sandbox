## 1. Purpose

Civic Continuum is exploring a limited review of one low-risk administrative workflow with a prospective business partner. The purpose is to understand the workflow, identify authorization and accountability gaps, and evaluate whether Maya Node's permission-boundary approach could be useful.

This is a human-led evaluation and discussion. It is not a live customer deployment or a promise that Maya Node will perform the partner's business tasks.

## 2. Proposed scope

The review may include:
1. **Workflow map:** A written outline of the selected process and its participants.
2. **Authorization review:** Identification of where approval is needed and how it is currently obtained.
3. **Failure scenarios:** Discussion of duplicate requests, expired approvals, revoked permissions, and unexpected errors.
4. **Sample receipts:** Review of available demonstration records, with their test environment and limitations clearly identified.
5. **Findings report:** Practical recommendations for improving workflow boundaries and human oversight.

Any demonstration or test must be separately agreed upon and remain within the verified capabilities of the selected environment.

## 5. Preliminary intake questions

**A. Workflow name and purpose**  
What administrative task would you like to examine?

**B. Current process**  
How is the task currently initiated, reviewed, and completed?

**C. Trigger and frequency**  
How often does it occur, and what starts the process?

**D. Important authorization points**  
Where should a person approve, review, or stop the task?

**E. Failure concerns**  
What would be the most concerning outcome if the process went wrong?

Please use fictional examples or general descriptions. Do not include personal or confidential business information.
