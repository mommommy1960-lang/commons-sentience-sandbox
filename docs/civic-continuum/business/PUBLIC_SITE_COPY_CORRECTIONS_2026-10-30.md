# Civic Continuum / Maya Node — Public Site Copy Corrections
**Prepared:** October 30, 2026  
**Status:** Review proposal only; no managed Site was edited or deployed.

## Why this change is needed

The inspected public pages currently describe more than one Maya product:
- The current managed Maya Node demonstration is a bounded text-file permission workflow.
- The older contact-site `/maya` page describes a one-hour / 10-reply experience and a planned 100-reply, 30-day offer.
- The browser-only `/demo` page describes fictional workflow simulations and correctly says it does not connect to a real organization, database, AI model, or alert service.

These are not interchangeable products. Public copy should identify each experience and avoid implying that the older reply-based offer is available.

## Page-by-page corrections

### 1. Contact-site Maya page
Current URL: https://civic-continuum-contact.freshcoin8.chatgpt.site/maya.html

**Replace the page's product description and planned reply-based pricing with:**

> **Maya Node — permission and action-record prototype**
>
> Maya Node is being developed to make a narrowly defined administrative request easier to review: what was requested, what a person authorized, what happened, and where human review is needed.
>
> The current demonstration is limited to a bounded text-file workflow. It is not a general-purpose chatbot, autonomous assistant, or live business-system integration.
>
> **Current availability:** exploratory conversations and founder-guided demonstrations using invented information only. No payment, live customer data, production access, or external automation is being offered.
>
> A possible future $25 evaluation has been discussed internally, but it is not an active offer. No checkout or payment is available.

Remove the “one hour / up to 10 replies” and “100 bounded Maya replies over 30 days” language. Those describe a different product concept.
