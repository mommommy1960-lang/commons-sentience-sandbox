# Civic Continuum Product Proof

## Purpose

Make one product genuinely usable before expanding the portfolio.

## Initial product lane

Maya Node is the first candidate because its public offer is narrow: a human-delivered consent and control design review for AI systems.

## Minimum usable offer

- a plain-language boundary-risk report;
- one adversarial test matched to the design;
- explicit limits: not certification, legal advice, penetration testing, or production-readiness approval;
- a documented intake, review, delivery, and correction process.

SAGE remains a protected concept and prototype track. No claim of production readiness or autonomous authority is made.

## Gate

Obtain real user feedback, document delivery time and failure modes, revise the offer, and only then expand into software or hardware.
