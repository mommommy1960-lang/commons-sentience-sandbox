# Civic Continuum Legislative Operating System

Status: **discussion-draft governance framework**. Nothing in this directory is enacted law, legal advice, a fiscal note, or legislative-counsel-approved statutory text.

This lane turns Civic Continuum values into bounded public-policy proposals. Its north star is consequential power that is attributable, bounded, auditable, interruptible, and correctable while protecting dignity, pluralism, consent, privacy, and the right to challenge a decision.

## First bounded pilot

`MODEL_BILL_001_FOOD_CONTINUITY.md` is a Washington discussion draft for a three-year Essential Food Continuity and Surplus Recovery Pilot. It starts with a narrow, testable problem: safely moving usable surplus food toward people before disposal, without forcing unsafe donations or shifting unfunded burdens onto small community organizations.

## Release gates

A proposal may not be labeled ready for introduction until it has named jurisdiction, counsel review, evidence and fiscal baselines, affected-community review, implementation ownership, appeal and correction paths, public reporting with privacy protection, independent evaluation, and a sunset or renewal gate.

Reality first. Anomaly second. Follow through.
