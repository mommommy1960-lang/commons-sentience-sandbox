## Renewals, taxes and costs not yet totaled

- Seattle: renew each calendar year by December 31. A late-2026 start can put a renewal soon after initial registration. The 2027 city rate is not verified here, so it is not silently treated as $73 or included in the initial subtotal. Revenue tier can change.
- LLC if selected: annual report $70 at its anniversary. A deferred initial report adds filing/possible online costs. Eligible self/noncommercial registered agent may cost $0, but a commercial agent needs a quote and public-address implications need review. No agent selected or identity/address published here.
- DOR: $5 renewal processing applies to applicable endorsement renewals, not an automatic annual UBI charge. Trade names remain registered until canceled. Endorsement fees are separate. State tax registration is $0 beyond the application process.
- WA sales tax and B&O: scope matters. IT services became retail-sales-taxable October 1, 2025; do not assume human delivery or a different price makes a service exempt. Use DOR classification and customer-location rules. Retailing B&O rate is 0.471% before applicable credits/deductions; sales tax is separate and not startup spending or retained revenue. No sales/expense ledger is available to calculate an amount due.
- Seattle B&O: 2026 threshold is $2 million with a standard deduction; required returns can still be due below threshold or with no activity. The tax and the annual license fee are different.
- Federal income and self-employment tax depend on actual profit and personal tax facts. The generally stated SE tax rate is 15.3%, subject to Schedule SE mechanics and thresholds; it is not 15.3% of all gross receipts in every case. No personal tax debt is calculated or published.
- Employer costs: L&I workers compensation, unemployment insurance, Paid Family and Medical Leave, WA Cares, payroll taxes, wages, paid leave and employment compliance require a separate workforce calculation if covered workers are engaged. Labeling someone a contractor does not itself remove coverage duties.
- School/bank deployments: request the buyer's real procurement, insurance, security, accessibility, data and contract requirements before buying audits. SOC 2 is a CPA examination/report; ISO certification is by external certification bodies. Neither is a universal government license. HIPAA has no HHS-recognized private certification shortcut. Applicability and implementation still matter.
- Hardware/manufacturing, regulated professional practice, food, devices, construction, child-facing products, multistate activity, product testing, laboratory work, equipment, rent, personal internet/ChatGPT subscriptions, tax arrears and dispute/litigation costs are outside this initial software/service budget. Run the official licensing wizard for actual activities. There is no fixed fee that makes every invention ready for sale.
