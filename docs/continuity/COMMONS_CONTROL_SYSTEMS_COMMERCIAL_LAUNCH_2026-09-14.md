## Commercial positioning

### Maya Node
A consent-and-control review discipline for AI-enabled products and workflows. Review dimensions include permission scope, instruction freshness, revocation, human override, auditability, and recovery paths.

### Commons MMR Audit
A governance resilience assessment built around Maximum Malicious Reach (MMR), Minimum Defensive Burden (MDB), Defensive Interruption Burden (DIB), concentration of authority, interruption paths, and recovery after harmful or unauthorized action.

### Reality Audit
A falsification-first workflow for extraordinary technical claims. Core sequence: claim -> null hypothesis -> established baseline -> confounders -> controls -> signal injection -> ablation -> preregistered thresholds -> repeated runs -> failure log -> claim classification.

### Human-Control Test
A low-cost self-guided entry product that leads customers toward the Maya Node Control Audit where deeper human review is needed.

## Claim discipline

The commercial products must not claim certification, legal advice, penetration testing, guaranteed compliance, medical treatment, propulsion validation, matter phasing, sentience, or any other capability not supported by evidence.

Use wording such as:
- design review
- governance assessment
- research workbook
- adversarial test
- evidence package
- control recommendation

Avoid wording such as:
- certified safe
- legally compliant
- unhackable
- production approved
- proven advanced propulsion
