# Civic Continuum Roadmap v2

**Status:** Public high-level operating roadmap  
**Version date:** 2026-09-18  
**Evidence boundary:** This roadmap organizes work. It does not prove civilization-scale feasibility, scientific validation, legal approval, product safety, revenue, or operational readiness.

## Purpose

Civic Continuum is being developed as a staged operating model for material security, accountable power, human control, local participation, lifelong learning, transparent evidence, graceful failure, and correction.

The immediate objective is to make a small, honest, usable version work now, then expand only when evidence, safety, resources, legal review, and community feedback justify the next stage.

## Operating rule

Reality first. Anomaly second. Follow through.

Every project must state:

1. the claim;
2. the intended user or affected community;
3. the current evidence status;
4. the next inspectable artifact;
5. cost and capacity requirements;
6. safety, privacy, rights, and legal gates;
7. a revenue or public-benefit path;
8. a stop, revise, rollback, or sunset condition.

## Three-layer structure

### Civic Core

Governance, rights, education, material security, anti-capture controls, public accountability, correction, and transition design.

### Product Layer

Bounded products and services that can be delivered and evaluated now, including Maya Node, SAGE software, Crowned Coil prototypes, governance tools, and small hospitality or education pilots.

### Research Layer

Reality Audit, orbital systems, propulsion, quantum engineering, biotechnology, and long-horizon infrastructure. Research remains labeled as research until independent evidence supports a narrower status.

## Evidence labels

- **PRODUCT:** usable offer with validated scope and a delivery path.
- **SERVICE:** human-delivered work with explicit boundaries.
- **PROTOTYPE:** working implementation or physical sample under test.
- **RESEARCH:** hypothesis, model, simulation, or literature-grounded proposal.
- **PUBLISHING:** rights-cleared educational or documentary release.
- **HOLD:** blocked by safety, evidence, legal, funding, privacy, or rights constraints.

A document, repository, timestamp, or DOI establishes a record. It does not by itself establish feasibility, safety, patent rights, enforceability, market demand, or scientific truth.

## Public release policy

Public repositories may include:

- mission and high-level roadmap;
- portfolio map and evidence labels;
- source links already public;
- test status and preserved negative results;
- limitations and correction history;
- sanitized governance templates;
- clear distinctions among products, prototypes, proposals, research, and fictional teaching devices.

Public repositories must exclude:

- credentials and secrets;
- personal addresses, phone numbers, medical information, and private correspondence;
- NDA documents and supplier quotes;
- unreleased patent strategy;
- production-ready private drawings or CAD;
- exploitable security details;
- unsafe medical, biological, chemical, weapons, or critical-infrastructure procedures;
- third-party confidential or unlicensed content.

## Release gates

No workstream is promoted without:

- a bounded claim;
- an inspectable artifact;
- a privacy and rights review;
- appropriate safety and security review;
- a reproducible test or documented review checklist;
- a failure and limitations log;
- an owner and maintenance path;
- a price, funding, or public-benefit decision;
- a correction, rollback, or sunset mechanism.

## Correspondence discipline

Read the complete thread before drafting. Reply only when there is a new unanswered request, a real deadline, an agreed follow-up date, or a complete invited package.

Silence is appropriate when no action is due. External messages, contracts, spending, private disclosure, and protected-branch merges require explicit authority and an evidence-backed reason.
