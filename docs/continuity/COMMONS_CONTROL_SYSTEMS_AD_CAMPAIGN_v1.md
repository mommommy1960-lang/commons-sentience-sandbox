## Short-form hook bank

- Your AI remembered permission. That does not mean it still has permission.
- If the emergency stop depends on the agent you're stopping, it is not an emergency stop.
- A capable AI is not automatically an authorized AI.
- What is the largest irreversible action one compromised account can take?
- Can your audit log explain a denial, or only a success?
- If your AI restarts, what authority wakes up with it?
