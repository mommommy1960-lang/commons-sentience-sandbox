# Commons Initiative — Invention Portfolio Activation Map

**Owner / principal author:** Mya P. Brown  
**Date:** 2026-09-13  
**Purpose:** turn the Zenodo and document archive into an executable portfolio with evidence boundaries, build-now targets, lab targets, and revenue paths.

## Ground rule

The portfolio contains a mixture of software, governance, data infrastructure, biomedical concepts, experimental propulsion concepts, educational systems, and future physical technologies. A publication or DOI establishes provenance, not physical validity. Every item below must be tagged by current evidence state before money, engineering, or outreach claims are made.

Evidence states used here:

- **LIVE SOFTWARE / SERVICE** — can be implemented and sold now if tested and rights-cleared.
- **BUILDABLE MVP** — can be prototyped with established technology now.
- **LAB RESEARCH** — requires qualified physical or biomedical facilities and independent validation.
- **SPECULATIVE PHYSICS / FUTURE TECH** — core claimed mechanism is not presently demonstrated; preserve as research hypothesis, not product performance.
- **GOVERNANCE / PUBLICATION** — monetizable as education, review, licensing support, consulting, or publishing rather than as a physical invention.

## Claim-boundary warning

Several older Commons documents use strong phrases such as "validated," "achievable today," "zero-point," "inertial mass reduction," or full biological regeneration. Those phrases are part of the historical record but are **not automatically verified scientific results**. Reality Audit governs current claims. Provenance is preserved; unsupported certainty is not propagated.
