# DC-Derived Works Rights Protection & Licensing Plan — 2026-09-14

**Scope:** The Pennyworth Papers, The Iceberg Table / Iceberg Lounge materials, Gotham After Midnight, and any other Commons creative work using DC-owned characters, settings, names, trademarks, or story-world elements.

## Core rule

Do **not** send full manuscripts, chapters, source files, recipes, story outlines, character treatments, artwork, or unpublished creative details through an unsolicited-submission form, fan-work upload, social channel, generic support channel, or any route whose terms grant broad use rights or disclaim confidentiality.

This rule exists because DC's current Terms of Use state that DC does not accept unsolicited submissions and that an unsolicited submission does not obligate DC to keep it confidential, refrain from using it, or compensate the sender. DC's fan-work submission guidance similarly warns that similarities to later DC work do not create an obligation to credit or pay the fan creator.

Public timestamping helps establish chronology and authorship of Mya P. Brown's original expression. It does **not** create ownership of DC's underlying characters, settings, trademarks, or continuity.

## Licensing path

Use a **rights/licensing inquiry**, not a creative submission.

The current Warner Bros. Discovery licensing information routes DC Comics / consumer-product licensing inquiries to:

**WBCPInquiries@wbd.com**

Initial contact should disclose only the minimum needed to determine whether an authorized licensing route exists:

- author: Mya P. Brown;
- independent creator / founder of The Commons Initiative;
- project type: original culinary/literary works built around DC-owned properties;
- titles only;
- requested rights: limited, non-exclusive print/e-book publication license for named works;
- territory, term, formats, approximate print/digital distribution if requested;
- request for the correct licensing process, submission protocol, and whether a low-cost, royalty-based, or no-advance structure is available for an independent creator;
- request for written confirmation of what materials they require and under what submission/confidentiality terms.

Do **not** attach manuscripts to the first inquiry.

## No-submission safety gate

Before any creative material leaves Commons custody, confirm in writing:

1. the recipient is authorized to evaluate licensing requests;
2. the submission is being requested by that authorized recipient;
3. the governing submission/licensing terms have been read and archived;
4. confidentiality / permitted-use terms are acceptable, or the risk has been consciously accepted;
5. the exact material to be sent has been inventoried and timestamped;
6. the version sent is preserved locally with a hash or immutable timestamp where practical;
7. no broader rights are granted than necessary for evaluation/licensing;
8. no statement falsely implies DC endorsement or official status before a signed license exists.

## Rights position to preserve

### Mya P. Brown / Commons interests

Preserve ownership and attribution in Mya P. Brown's original expression, including original prose, original arrangement, original recipes/instructions to the extent protectable, original commentary, original scenes, original analysis, original illustrations/artwork where applicable, and other independently created material.

### DC / third-party rights

DC/WBD retains rights in its preexisting characters, names, locations, trademarks, logos, continuity, story-world elements, and other protected material.

Commercial publication using those protected DC elements should not proceed unless a defensible legal basis exists, preferably a written license from the authorized rights holder.

## Preferred deal posture

Request, where available:

- non-exclusive publication rights;
- print + e-book rights only unless broader rights are specifically desired;
- limited term with renewal option;
- defined territory;
- no assignment of Mya P. Brown's original manuscript ownership;
- no transfer of unrelated Commons IP;
- royalty-based or no-advance structure if possible;
- clear approval process for title, cover, trademarks, and marketing language;
- clear accounting/payment terms;
- reversion/termination provisions;
- written attribution terms.

Any proposed license should be reviewed carefully before signature. This operational plan is not legal advice.
