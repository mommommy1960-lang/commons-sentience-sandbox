## Formalization actions still required

### 1. Washington LLC formation

Target entity name: **The Commons Initiative LLC**, subject to state name availability.

Required action:
- File a Washington Certificate of Formation with the Washington Secretary of State.
- Include the Initial Report with formation where available.
- Select a registered agent and principal office.
- Preserve the filed formation confirmation and entity number in the corporate record.

Current state fee published by Washington SOS: **$180 plus the online processing fee**. Expedited service is optional and should not be purchased unless necessary.

### 2. EIN

After formation:
- Obtain an EIN directly from the IRS.
- Do not pay a third party for the EIN application.
- Store the EIN confirmation in a private corporate records location, not a public repository.

### 3. Washington business license / UBI

After the Secretary of State filing:
- File the Washington Business License Application with Department of Revenue / Business Licensing Service.
- Current new-business application processing fee: **$50**, plus any endorsements / trade-name fees.
- Preserve UBI and license confirmations privately.

### 4. Seattle business license tax certificate

Because the initiative operates from Seattle, confirm applicability and register where required.

For 2026, Seattle lists the Tier 1 annual business license tax certificate fee at **$73**, with a half-year first-year reduction for a business starting July 1 or later where applicable.

## Partner / manufacturing gate

Before any vendor moves beyond quote / evaluation into paid engineering or manufacture:

1. NDA if confidential information will be disclosed.
2. Written scope of work.
3. Background-IP schedule identifying Commons pre-existing IP.
4. Project-IP ownership / assignment terms.
5. Tooling ownership and access terms.
6. Source-file / CAD / Gerber / BOM delivery requirements.
7. Confidentiality and subcontractor flow-down.
8. No independent manufacture, resale, licensing, white-label use, or derivative commercialization without written permission.
9. Payment milestones tied to deliverables.
10. Termination, rework, warranty, and dispute terms.

Public policy reference:
https://github.com/mommommy1960-lang/maya-node/blob/main/docs/PARTNER_IP_AND_MANUFACTURING_BOUNDARIES.md

## Business-infrastructure principle

The business record should become as inspectable as the research record:

- claims must match evidence,
- authority must be traceable,
- rights must be explicit,
- outside partners receive only the permissions required for their scope,
- research provenance and company ownership must remain distinguishable,
- no partner receives implied ownership merely by helping build, review, manufacture, or distribute a product.
