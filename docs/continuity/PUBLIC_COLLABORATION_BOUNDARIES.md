# Collaboration and evaluation boundaries
Civic Continuum welcomes rigorous peer review and collaboration. We begin with a defined purpose, synthetic data, separate accounts and observable acceptance criteria.

- Viewing or testing a public demo does not grant company ownership, equity, financial authority or administrative access.
- Existing licenses, including the MIT license for covered Commons sandbox material, continue to govern that material. This notice does not revoke public or third-party rights.
- Protected information is shared only after appropriate identity, scope and written confidentiality review. No credentials or customer data belong in a peer test.
- Code contributions, jointly created inventions, commercial integrations and paid work require their own written rights and scope agreements before work starts.
- Branding, case studies and named comparative results require specific approval where legally applicable; lawful reporting and nonwaivable rights remain protected.
- We preserve results and failures, and distinguish peer evaluation from certification, partnership and production readiness.

These are our prospective operating rules, not a contract imposed on every reader or a guarantee against misuse. Our detailed existing [IP and partner protection policy](COMMONS_IP_AND_PARTNER_PROTECTION_POLICY.md) remains in effect, subject to applicable licenses and law.

## Mutual protection across Civic Continuum work

These operating rules apply prospectively to Civic Continuum collaborations involving research, inventions, software, services, demonstrations, marketing and business development. Each engagement still needs terms suited to its actual activities and legal parties. No claim is made that every repository, account or service has already been audited.

We respect collaborators' confidential material, ownership and authorized boundaries just as we expect them to respect ours. Neither side receives company ownership or unrestricted rights merely by helping. Rights in actual contributions must be resolved under applicable law, existing licenses and any signed agreement.

| Stage | Required boundary before advancing |
|---|---|
| Public discussion or peer demo | Defined purpose, public or synthetic materials, separate accounts; public licenses continue to apply. |
| Confidential exchange | Verified legal parties and authority, appropriate mutual NDA, permitted purpose, approved recipients and disclosure list. |
| Code, inventions or shared deliverables | Separate written scope, background asset schedule, contribution and results ownership/use terms, third-party license review and exit plan. |
| Production or commercial work | Agreed delivery, payment, liability, data handling and incident terms; specific minimum access with an end date. |
| Publication or exit | Specific permissions where needed, accurate evidence and attribution, access removal, appropriate return/deletion and retained-record rules. |

## Where these protections belong

- **Public GitHub and LinkedIn:** the collaboration boundaries and evidence-based status. This page is linked from the repository README and our [LinkedIn announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7517625914004815872/).
- **Private business records:** asset ownership and license inventory, due diligence, agreement versions, signatures, disclosure approvals, access register and incident records. Do not publish confidential designs, secrets or private negotiating details.
- **Each engagement:** the relevant agreed terms before protected exchange or work. A general notice, social post or NDA alone is not a development contract, patent filing, security configuration or assignment of rights.
- **Accounts and services:** implement permissions in the actual system and retain evidence of granting, expiry and revocation. A written rule does not prove a technical control exists.
- **Websites, repositories and service onboarding:** place a visible policy link at the relevant entry point; review each product's license, privacy, consumer and service terms for its actual function. This page does not silently replace existing terms.

Implementation begins with the verified public notices and a private unsigned evaluation draft. Broader account reviews, website placement, tailored contracts and legal review remain tracked work; they are not represented as complete.

## Basis for the approach

[WIPO's technology transfer agreement guidance](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/technology-transfer/agreements) distinguishes confidentiality arrangements from agreements governing collaborative work and IP rights. [UK Intellectual Property Office NDA guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/non-disclosure-agreements/non-disclosure-agreements) describes mutual confidentiality where both parties disclose. These are drafting resources; UK guidance is not a substitute for advice on the applicable US jurisdiction.
