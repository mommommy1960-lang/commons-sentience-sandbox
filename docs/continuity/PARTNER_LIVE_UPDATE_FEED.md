## Start here if you can help

The Commons Initiative has reached a point where several important technical questions can no longer be advanced responsibly by more software simulation alone. The next meaningful Flux/Aurora milestone is a **calibrated, low-energy physical falsification experiment** with independent force and reaction-force measurement, synchronized environmental instrumentation, frozen analysis rules, sham/reversal controls, and eventually independent replication.

The software, reality-audit framework, one-million-trial synthetic false-positive study, validation manual, physical bench readiness package, calibration/uncertainty plan, blind-control protocol, procurement tiers, and CAL-00 exit criteria are now public and traceable in GitHub.

**What is stopping the next physical milestone:** access to calibrated instrumentation, low-energy bench/lab access, fixture fabrication, independent experimental review/replication, and the money or in-kind support required to obtain those things.

**What support pays for:** measurement quality, calibration, bench access, instrumentation, fixture materials, independent review, and the ability to run a decisive experiment whose result is useful whether it is positive or null. Funding does **not** purchase a desired scientific conclusion.

If you are a laboratory, university team, metrology specialist, aerospace/controls engineer, instrument manufacturer, funder, or technically qualified independent reviewer, in-kind access can be as valuable as cash. A calibrated instrument loan, bench time, fixture fabrication, measurement review, or blinded replication path can close a real blocker.

The broader purpose of The Commons Initiative is to develop systems, research, products, and governance structures that make human well-being, accountable technology, evidence, and long-term species flourishing the design target rather than an afterthought. The work is ambitious; the public record is intended to make it inspectable rather than merely inspirational.

This page is the shortest public route to the current state of The Commons Initiative. It is intentionally curated: implementation, testing, simulation, physical measurement, publication, and independent verification remain separate evidence states.

These are measurement-system requirements, not predicted Flux thrust.
