# Crowned Coil Prototype Program

## Purpose

This document turns the Crowned Coil brand package and manufacturer communications into a controlled physical-prototype program. It records what is proposed, what has been requested from manufacturers, and what must be verified before any product is called safe, finished, or ready for sale.

- A private-label snake-hook prototype quotation was requested from Reptile Supply Company on September 13, 2026.

- A custom silicone reptile-training-target prototype quotation was requested from YMS on September 13, 2026.

- A manufacturer replied with company information and requested NDA contracting-party information on September 15, 2026.

These records document outreach and design intent. They do not establish that a prototype has been manufactured, delivered, or safety-tested.

## Prototype A Snake hook

### Intended purpose

A branded reptile-handling hook for supervised keeper use.

### Design direction

- Smooth, balanced handle
- Safe, rounded hook geometry
- Corrosion-resistant material
- Non-slip grip
- Crowned Coil visual identity
- Optional interchangeable grip sleeve or decorative accent

### Required manufacturer deliverables

1. Written quotation
2. Material specification
3. Dimensional drawing or CAD preview
4. Minimum order quantity
5. Prototype lead time
6. Unit pricing at prototype and small-batch volumes
7. Finish and color options
8. Packaging options
9. Statement of intended use and limitations

### Safety verification before release

- No sharp or snagging edges
- No exposed coating that flakes during ordinary use
- Grip remains stable when handled with clean, dry hands
- Hook geometry does not create an avoidable pinch or entanglement hazard
- Cleaning and inspection instructions are documented
- Use is supervised and consistent with qualified reptile-handling guidance

## Prototype B Silicone training target

### Intended purpose

A soft, washable target object for controlled reptile-training and enrichment demonstrations. It is not a live-animal substitute, feeding object, or unrestricted chew toy.

### Design direction

- Soft crescent-moon silhouette
- Fairy Godmother theme
- Non-toxic, manufacturer-certified silicone or another validated material
- Smooth, cleanable surface
- No detachable decorative parts
- Size and weight documented before testing

### Required manufacturer deliverables

1. Material and additive disclosure
2. Intended-use certification or test documentation
3. Shore hardness or equivalent specification
4. Dimensions and mass
5. Colorant and finish information
6. Prototype mold or tooling cost
7. Sample approval process
8. Cleaning and storage instructions
9. Warnings and use limitations

### Safety verification before release

- No detachable parts
- No accessible magnets, beads, glitter, or loose ornaments
- No sharp seams or flash
- No unsafe odor or visible material degradation
- Cleaning process does not damage the material
- Supervised use only until independent handling review is complete

## Packaging concept

The initial package may include:

- Snake hook or training target
- Velvet or protective pouch
- Plain-language care and safety card
- Product identification and batch record
- Founder or edition card only if it does not imply certification
- No claim of veterinary endorsement unless independently obtained

Luxury packaging is a presentation concept. It must not obscure safety instructions or replace product testing.

## Stage gates

### Gate 1 Design brief

Complete when dimensions, materials, intended use, exclusions, and safety questions are written.

### Gate 2 Manufacturer quotation

Complete when a written quote and deliverables are received.

### Gate 3 Prototype sample

Complete when a physical sample is received and photographed with its identifying information.

### Gate 4 Bench inspection

Complete when fit, finish, cleaning, durability, and foreseeable misuse checks are recorded.

### Gate 5 Independent handling review

Complete when a qualified independent reviewer evaluates the product for its intended reptile-handling context.

### Gate 6 Limited pilot

Complete only after the previous gates pass and use is supervised with documented feedback.

### Gate 7 Release decision

Complete only after the product claims, warnings, packaging, pricing, and fulfillment plan are supported by records.
