## What remains

1. **Collect measurements.** Fill in the hook and target dimensions, weights, and any available reference measurements.
2. **Wait for supplier replies.** Obtain written quotes, exact materials, dimensions, construction methods, lead times, shipping, MOQ, and total costs.
3. **Compare the quotes.** Record each response in the supplier tracker and quote comparison template.
4. **Review safety documentation.** Confirm the exact silicone, pigments, coatings, metals, finishes, and any available test or composition documents.
5. **Review ownership terms.** Check NDA language, drawing ownership, tooling ownership, storage, reuse, and permission to use samples or images.
6. **Approve the prototype drawing.** Resolve all blank dimensions and reject any design with glued, loose, sharp, shedding, or detachable decorative parts.
7. **Authorize a sample only after review.** Approve paid prototype work only after the written quote and terms are acceptable.
8. **Inspect and test the sample.** Start with non-animal inspection: dimensions, finish, cleaning, grip, balance, durability, snagging, shedding, and detachable-part checks.
9. **Complete independent review before launch.** Any supervised animal-contact evaluation, public safety claim, production batch, or sale must wait for adequate evidence and independent review.

## Non-negotiable design rule

Luxury decoration must be engraved, molded, embedded, sealed, coated, or structurally integrated. No glued rhinestones, loose jewels, loose glitter, dangling charms, sharp hardware, flaking paint, or detachable caps.

## Evidence boundary

The concept and documentation are applied. Physical safety, supplier feasibility, material suitability, prototype performance, independent review, and sales readiness remain unverified until evidence is collected.
