## Product identity

This kit is a reptile-handling and training accessory. It is not a toy, chew item, feeding utensil, or substitute for experienced animal handling.

## Safe-use instructions

- Inspect the hook, grip, target, and storage components before every use.
- Do not use the product if any edge is sharp, any surface is peeling, or any component is loose, cracked, or damaged.
- Use calm, supervised, non-feeding handling practices.
- Keep the target and hook away from chewing, feeding, and unsupervised access.
- Stop immediately if the animal shows distress, snagging, striking risk, or other unsafe behavior.
- Clean only according to the final material-specific instructions.
- Store out of reach of children and animals when not in use.

## Decoration and materials

Decorative details must be molded, engraved, embedded, sealed, coated, or structurally integrated. No glued stones, loose jewels, loose glitter, dangling charms, sharp hardware, or detachable decorative parts are permitted.

Final material, coating, cleaning, and safety statements must be completed from the approved supplier documentation. Do not print unverified claims such as pet-safe, non-toxic, or load-rated.

## Emergency and limitation statement

This product does not replace veterinary advice, professional training, or the keeper's judgment. Discontinue use and inspect the product after any unexpected contact, drop, snag, or damage.
