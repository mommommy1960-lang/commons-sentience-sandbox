Build carefully. Test in public. Repair what fails.

This is a public starting point for Civic Continuum and its evidence records. It documents the project without claiming sentience, consciousness, autonomy, revenue, or hardware readiness.

01 / BOUNDARIES

Consent first

Actions and interactions should be visible, understandable, and within the authority a person actually granted.

02 / CONTINUITY

Context matters

Useful assistance should preserve relevant context without quietly turning memory into permission.

03 / REPAIR

Failures are signals

We keep a record of what was applied, what was tested, and what still needs independent review.

Production portfolio

One body of work. Different readiness levels.

The portfolio includes services, software, books, physical products, research, and civic systems. Each moves through the evidence gate appropriate to it.

Public demonstration

Consent and governance workflows

Maya Node demonstrates bounded permission and revocation behavior. Paid delivery and broader integration are separate readiness gates.

Preparing for release

Books, briefs, and curricula

Original manuscripts move through rights, privacy, evidence, editorial, accessibility, and format review before sale.

Prototype path

SAGE, Crowned Coil, and physical concepts

These require conventional engineering, supplier scope, safety testing, and independent review before customer use.

Research only

Reality Audit, Aurora, and Flux

Simulation, measurement, and falsification work remains explicitly separate from claims of validated propulsion or new physics.

A DOI, concept image, simulation, or design document records a publication or research artifact; it does not by itself prove a product is safe, manufacturable, effective, or ready for sale.
