A clear beginning · mutual respect

Before we collaborate.

We welcome useful criticism, thoughtful introductions and rigorous peer review. Let’s protect each other’s work from the start.

Our starting scope

  • Public information, public demos and harmless synthetic examples.
  • Separate accounts. No shared credentials, private designs, customer records or financial access.
  • Each person keeps their existing rights. Existing public licenses still apply.
  • No ownership, exclusivity, payment commitment or partnership is created by an introduction.
Read the full mutual collaboration policy on GitHub ↗

When we move beyond public discussion

Before intentional confidential disclosure, we verify the parties and agree appropriate confidentiality terms. Before code contributions, shared inventions, private access or commercial work, we agree the actual scope, rights, responsibilities and exit terms. Either side can decline or keep the discussion public.

A page visit, a click, silence or a friendly greeting is not treated here as a signed NDA or a completed collaboration agreement. This notice does not remove rights already granted under a license or restrict lawful protected reporting.

Our standard introduction

Hello, and thank you for connecting with Civic Continuum. We’d be glad to hear what you’re working on. Before we explore a collaboration, please read our mutual boundaries: https://github.com/mommommy1960-lang/commons-sentience-sandbox/blob/main/docs/continuity/PUBLIC_COLLABORATION_BOUNDARIES.md Let’s start with public information, harmless synthetic examples and separate accounts. Please confirm that this starting scope works for you. We’ll agree appropriate separate written terms before confidential exchange, shared development, private access or paid work. We’ll respect your work and rights in return.

Confirm the starting scope

If you want to propose a peer evaluation, tell us the public workflow you want to discuss and confirm that we’ll keep the initial exchange public or synthetic. Please do not include secrets or private designs. Sending an acknowledgment records that limited discussion scope; it does not replace any later agreement.

Privacy on this page

This static page has no collaboration-submission form, visitor identity register or added analytics. An email is sent only if you choose to send it in your own email app. Do not put confidential material in that email. The site host and email providers may process their ordinary service data; this page does not promise anonymous browsing or confidential intake.

The home-page welcome notice remembers only that its current version was dismissed in this browser. That local preference is not a signature, identity check or message to Civic Continuum. Use the policy link in every new business introduction, and ask for an explicit reply before starting a peer evaluation.
