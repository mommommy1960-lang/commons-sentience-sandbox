# Civic Continuum outreach instructions

When a task involves Civic Continuum introductions, networking, collaborator replies or business outreach, first read [the first contact protection protocol](docs/continuity/FIRST_CONTACT_PROTECTION_PROTOCOL.md) and the [public collaboration boundaries](docs/continuity/PUBLIC_COLLABORATION_BOUNDARIES.md).

Include the policy at the first substantive business exchange, request explicit confirmation of the public/synthetic/separate-account starting scope, and record actual acknowledgment privately. Treat protections as mutual. No private material, access, shared development or financial/ownership commitment before the appropriate separate verified terms and authorization. Respect existing licenses and lawful reporting rights. A view, notice click, silence or unsent draft is not a signature or acknowledgment.

For existing contacts, use the next substantive exchange and check prior receipts to avoid duplicate messages. Do not infer permission for unattended bulk outreach. Preserve requested pauses and do not publish private contact logs. Source and test claims must remain evidence-based. Ordinary code changes do not require contacting anyone or accepting a contract.

## Business budget and existing assets

Before recommending purchases or responding to “I just got paid / what can we pay today?”, read the [current startup-cost and existing-asset record](docs/continuity/CIVIC_CONTINUUM_STARTUP_COSTS_2026-10-18.md) and any accessible private Money Available Playbook/current receipts. Reuse the existing civiccontinuum.org domain, contact infrastructure, websites and current EIN; do not recommend duplicate purchases. Confirm designated available money, actual unpaid costs, existing registrations, current fee schedules and free assistance before an affordable plan. Pending grants are not cash. Keep actual cash allocations and full receipts private. The public Pay today workbook is a blank template, not spending authority or proof that licenses are issued.

## IP and marketing workflow
Before publication, sharing designs, adding contributors, using AI marketing tools or making protection/security claims, read [IP, security and marketing practices](docs/continuity/IP_SECURITY_AND_MARKETING_PRACTICES.md). Inventory assets privately, verify rights/licenses and disclosure history, use the release checklist, and preserve test evidence. Do not disclose unpublished inventions or claim universal security. Keep actual registrations, signatures and renewals distinct from proposed protections.
