# Civic Continuum outreach instructions

When a task involves Civic Continuum introductions, networking, collaborator replies or business outreach, first read [the first contact protection protocol](docs/continuity/FIRST_CONTACT_PROTECTION_PROTOCOL.md) and the [public collaboration boundaries](docs/continuity/PUBLIC_COLLABORATION_BOUNDARIES.md).

Include the policy at the first substantive business exchange, request explicit confirmation of the public/synthetic/separate-account starting scope, and record actual acknowledgment privately. Treat protections as mutual. No private material, access, shared development or financial/ownership commitment before the appropriate separate verified terms and authorization. Respect existing licenses and lawful reporting rights. A view, notice click, silence or unsent draft is not a signature or acknowledgment.

For existing contacts, use the next substantive exchange and check prior receipts to avoid duplicate messages. Do not infer permission for unattended bulk outreach. Preserve requested pauses and do not publish private contact logs. Source and test claims must remain evidence-based. Ordinary code changes do not require contacting anyone or accepting a contract.
